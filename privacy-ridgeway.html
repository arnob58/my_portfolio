Privacy Policy for Ridgeway Cargo

Effective date: October 19, 2026

KeramotiStudio ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Ridgeway Cargo mobile game.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use Ridgeway Cargo.

1. Information We Collect

Ridgeway Cargo does not require you to create an account, and we do not directly request personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

The game stores certain gameplay information locally on your device, such as game progress, settings, vehicles, purchases or entitlements, and photos taken in photo mode. This local data is not sent to us.

2. Analytics

Ridgeway Cargo uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played and to improve it.

Firebase Analytics may automatically collect information such as app installs and opens, sessions, gameplay events (for example distance driven, deliveries, crashes and cargo damage in the game, and playtime), device model, operating system and app version, language, approximate country or region derived from your IP address, and an app instance identifier.

We use this information in aggregate to measure usage and retention, find crashes and performance problems, improve gameplay, and measure whether our advertising campaigns bring new players. We do not use it to identify individual players.

Analytics is enabled by default. A notice on the welcome screen informs you of this, and you can opt out at any time (see section 8).

3. Advertising

Ridgeway Cargo uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect or process information such as device and advertising identifiers, IP address, app and advertisement interactions, and diagnostic or performance information.

We also use Google Ads to promote Ridgeway Cargo. Google Ads may use Firebase Analytics data, such as first app opens, to measure the performance of these campaigns.

This information may be used by Google for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and advertising measurement.

More information about Google's data practices is available at: Google's Privacy & Terms .

4. In-App Purchases

Ridgeway Cargo offers an optional "Remove Ads" purchase through Google Play.

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not directly receive or store your payment card or banking information.

The game may verify your purchase entitlement through Google Play so purchased features can be provided or restored.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party services used by the game, including Google Firebase Analytics, Google AdMob, Google Ads, and Google Play, according to their respective privacy policies and terms. Google's Privacy Policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

We do not share information with anyone else unless required by law.

6. Data Retention

Analytics event data is kept by Google Firebase Analytics for the retention period set in our Google Analytics settings (no longer than 14 months) and is then deleted. Aggregated reports that do not identify individual users may be kept longer.

Data stored locally on your device remains until you delete it, for example by uninstalling the game or clearing its storage.

7. Children's Privacy

Ridgeway Cargo is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Your Choices

Opt out of analytics: You can turn off analytics at any time in the game's settings. When you opt out, the game stops sending analytics data, stops sharing data for advertising measurement, and resets its analytics data on your device.

Advertising settings: You can manage advertising-related privacy settings, reset or delete your advertising identifier, and opt out of personalized ads through your Android device and Google account settings.

Remove ads: You may purchase the optional Remove Ads feature to disable advertisements within Ridgeway Cargo.

Delete local data: Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes your local save, settings, and photos.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Canada's PIPEDA, the EU or UK GDPR, or California privacy law), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to withdraw your consent. Because we do not hold your name or contact details, the simplest way to stop collection is to opt out in the game's settings. For other requests, contact us at the email below.

10. Data Security

We rely on established platform and service providers for analytics, advertising, and payment functionality. Data sent to these services is encrypted in transit. However, no electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Ridgeway Cargo's features, third-party services, or legal requirements change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date.
