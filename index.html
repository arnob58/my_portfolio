02 / SYSTEMS THINKING

Pretty is not
a strategy.

The work was organized around audience needs, funnel intent, topic adjacency, and repeatable content architecture.

WHY

Three content tiers

50%

Growth — earn organic attention and address broad housing frustrations without aggressive sales language.

15%

Action — conversion-led content tied to live buying moments, launches, and releases.

35%

Community — deepen trust, brand equity, and design transparency.

HOW

Three operating modes

  • EvergreenChallenge status-quo assumptions and recurring homeownership myths.
  • CampaignsTime-bound product stories aligned with development phases.
  • ReactiveUse relevance + authority filters to respond to market, economic, and zoning shifts.
WHAT

Adjacent possible

Move from literal product features into broader audience-aware themes.
